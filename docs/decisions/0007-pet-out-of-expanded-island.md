# 0007: The squad pet stays out of the expanded Dynamic Island

Status: **proposed**, awaiting the owner.

Affects: M1 (shipped this way), M5 (the pet).

## Context

SPEC.md §1.3, expanded presentation: *"up to 4 squad members as creatures with names and their own
countdowns; squad pet with progress bar; buttons: Pause, End, Cheer."*

## Finding

The owner's M0 review called the island's spacing "really weird". The expanded island is about
160 pt tall at most, and the camera sits in the middle of its top edge. Your Lockling and time,
the squad name, four squadmates with names and timers, a pet row and three buttons don't fit
without shrinking everything below legibility. Apple's own expanded activities show one idea
with at most one row of detail.

## Proposal

- Expanded island: you and your time, the squad name, and either your buttons (solo) or your
  squadmates (squad). Cheer joins the buttons in M3.
- The pet lives on the Lock Screen card (where it is now), in StandBy, and in the Home and Lock
  Screen widgets (M6).
- SPEC.md §1.3's expanded bullet changes to match once the owner agrees.
