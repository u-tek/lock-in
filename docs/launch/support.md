## What is Lockin?

Lockin is a focus timer you share with friends. Start a session and your timer sits in your
Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen. When your squad locks in together, everyone's live timer
shows up there too, so you can see who's still going without opening anything. Your squad raises
a pet together, and it only grows when you all stay focused.

Lockin needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. It works great solo, and you don't need an account
for that.

## Live Activities and the Dynamic Island

### My timer isn't showing in the Dynamic Island or on the Lock Screen

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app → **Lockin** → **Live Activities**, and turn it on.
2. In **Settings → Face ID & Passcode** (or **Touch ID & Passcode**), under *Allow Access When
   Locked*, make sure **Live Activities** is on.
3. Start a new session in Lockin. A session that was already running when you changed the setting
   won't appear until you start another.

If Live Activities are off, Lockin shows a reminder on the session screen with a button to take you
there.

On iPhones without a Dynamic Island, your timer shows on the Lock Screen and as a banner at the top
of the screen instead. That's how iOS works for every app.

### My friends' timers aren't updating

Countdowns tick on your phone by themselves. Only changes, such as someone joining, pausing,
finishing or leaving, come over the internet, usually within a few seconds. If those are slow:

1. **Settings → Lockin → Live Activities → More Frequent Updates**: turn it on. This lets iOS
   deliver squad updates sooner.
2. **Settings → Notifications → Lockin**: make sure notifications are allowed.
3. Check you're online. Low Power Mode and a weak connection can delay updates.
4. Open Lockin. While it's open, the app updates the timers straight from our server.

iOS also limits how many updates any app can send in an hour. If your squad is very busy (lots of
pausing and cheering), a few updates may arrive late. Timers always stay correct.

### What's desk mode?

Put your iPhone on a charger, on its side, and lock it. With StandBy turned on (**Settings →
StandBy**), your Lockin session fills the screen like a desk clock: your timer, or your whole
squad's, big enough to see from across the room. If you only see a small bubble, tap it.

## Friends and squads

### How do I add friends?

Open **Friends** in Lockin. You can:

- **share your code or link** with a friend (in a message, on social, wherever you like);
- **show your QR code** for them to scan;
- **enter their code** if they've sent you one.

Codes last 7 days, so make a new one if yours has run out. There's no search and no contact upload,
so people can only add you if you give them your code.

You need to sign in with Apple to add friends. It takes one tap, and we never see your Apple
password or email.

### How do squads work?

A squad is a small group that locks in together.

- **Making one:** create a squad, give it a name and invite people with its code, link or QR code.
- **Size:** a squad has 2 to 4 people, or up to 6 with Lockin Plus. You can be in 1 squad, or up to
  3 with Plus.
- **Locking in together:** when someone starts a squad session, the others get a "locking in"
  notification and can join at any time while it's running. Everyone picks their own length. Each
  person's timer shows in everyone's Dynamic Island and on their Lock Screen.
- **Cheers:** tap a squadmate to send a quick emoji cheer. It pops up on their Live Activity for a
  moment. There's no chat.
- **The squad pet** hatches from an egg after your first group session and grows with every
  minute you focus together. It's happiest when people have focused today.
- **Streaks:** a day counts when at least half the squad finishes a session of 20 minutes or more.
- **Leaderboard:** your squad's focused minutes for the week. It resets every Monday.

If you end early or pause for too long, your Lockling shows it to the squad and the pet's mood dips
a little. It's a nudge, not a punishment.

### Can I stop the "locking in" notifications?

Yes. Open the squad and turn on **Mute**: you'll stop getting its "locking in" notifications, and
you can still join its sessions. [OWNER: once quiet hours ship, add: "Quiet hours (10 pm to 7 am by
default) keep things silent overnight. Change them in Settings."] You can also turn off Lockin's
notifications completely in **Settings → Notifications → Lockin**.

### How do I cancel?

Apple manages Plus subscriptions. On your iPhone, open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions →
Lockin Plus → Cancel Subscription**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the
next charge. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for. If you're on the free
trial, cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

Lifetime is a one-off purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

### I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it

Open Lockin → **Settings → Lockin Plus → Restore**. Use the same Apple Account you bought it
with. Restoring is free.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law aren't affected.

## Your account

### How do I delete my account?

In Lockin, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This deletes your profile, friends,
squad memberships, session history, coins and cosmetics from our server, and disconnects Lockin
from your Apple Account. Squads you made carry on for the other members. It can't be undone.

**Deleting your account doesn't cancel Lockin Plus.** Cancel it in **Settings → [your name] →
Subscriptions** first.

More detail is in our [privacy policy](/privacy).

### How do I change my name or handle?

**Settings → Account**. Names and handles go through a filter, so some words aren't allowed.

## Safety

### How do I report or block someone?

- **A friend:** open **Friends**, swipe left on them, then tap **Report** or **Block**.
- **Someone in your squad:** open the squad, tap their name, then tap **Report** or **Block**.
  [OWNER: check that this exists in the app at launch; see the open questions in the launch
  checklist.]

Blocking someone removes them as a friend. They can't add you again or join your squads, and we
don't tell them. You can unblock people in **Settings → Account → Blocked**.

When you report someone, we look at it within [OWNER: e.g. 24 hours]. We may change or remove a
name, and suspend or delete an account that breaks our [terms](/terms).
We never tell anyone who reported them.

If someone is in danger, contact the police on **000** (in Australia). If you're worried about
something that happened online, [eSafety](https://www.esafety.gov.au) can help.
