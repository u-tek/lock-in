## The short version

- **Solo doesn't need an account.** If you never sign in, your Lockling, sessions, coins and streak
  stay on your phone.
- **Friends need Sign in with Apple.** Once you sign in, we keep what your friends and squad need
  to see you: your handle, display name, Lockling and timers.
- **We don't collect** your location, your contacts or your email address. There's no chat, so
  there are no messages to collect. We don't advertise, we don't track you across other apps or
  websites, and we never sell your information.
- **App analytics are counts only**, like "a session was completed". They never include names,
  anything you typed, or anything about your friends.
- **You can delete your account in the app** at any time. That deletes your data from our server.

## What we collect

### If you use Lockin without an account

Everything stays on your iPhone: your Lockling, session history, coins, streak and settings. They're
kept in the app's own storage on your phone, which Lockin's Live Activity and widgets can read too.
Our server never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Even without an account, two things leave your phone: the anonymous counts described under
"Analytics", and any Lockin Plus purchase, which Apple handles.

### Analytics: counts only

To learn what's working, the app counts a small, fixed list of things:

- onboarding steps (started, Lockling picked, notifications allowed or declined);
- sessions completed (with the number of minutes and the number of people in it), and your first
  completed session;
- Live Activities started, failed, or switched off in Settings;
- squads created, invites shared, invites redeemed and squad sessions joined;
- the app being opened, so we can see how many people come back after 1, 7 and 30 days;
- the Plus screen being shown, free trials started and purchases made.

Each count is an event name with, at most, a few whole numbers attached (like 25 minutes, or 3
people). The app is built so that names, handles, squad names, your friends' IDs and anything you
type can't be attached to them.

## What we don't collect

- **Location.** None, ever. Not even the Live Activity can access it.
- **Contacts.** We never upload your address book. You add friends by code, QR code or link only.
- **Your email address.** We don't ask Apple for it and we don't store one.
- **Messages.** Lockin has no chat. Cheers are a fixed set of emoji.
- **Payment details.** Apple handles payments. We never see your card or bank details.
- **Advertising and tracking.** There are no ads in Lockin and no advertising identifiers. We don't
  track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use data brokers.

**We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.**

## Deleting your account

In Lockin, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. When you confirm:

- we delete your profile (handle, display name, Lockling), friends, blocks, invite codes, devices
  and push tokens, session history, coins, cosmetics and squad memberships from our server;
- squads you created carry on for the other members, without you;
- squad sessions you were part of stay in the other members' history, without you;
- reports you sent stay, with your name removed, so we can still act on them;
- we tell Apple to revoke Lockin's Sign in with Apple access for your account.

Your account is gone straight away. Our hosting provider's database restore history keeps a copy
for up to [OWNER: confirm the restore window, e.g. 30] days, and then that's gone too.

**Deleting your account doesn't cancel Lockin Plus.** Apple bills subscriptions, so cancel in your
iPhone's **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** first. Anything stored only on your phone stays
there until you delete the app.
