# M8 — Deep Focus

Status: **in progress** (built ahead of Apple's approval; switched off until it arrives)

## Scope

SPEC.md §5.8, Plus only: pick distracting apps with the Family Controls picker; during a session
they're shielded; "Open anyway" on a shield records a **slip**, which the squad sees (member state
5) and which dents the pet's mood.

## How it fits together

| Piece | Where | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `DeepFocus` module | `Packages/LKKit/Sources/DeepFocus` | Authorisation, the saved app selection, starting and stopping shields for a session, the slip inbox in the App Group |
| Shield configuration | `DeepFocusExtensions/ShieldConfiguration` | Draws the shield: the user's Lockling, "You're locked in", "Back to focus", "Open anyway" |
| Shield action | `DeepFocusExtensions/ShieldAction` | "Back to focus" closes the app; "Open anyway" records a slip and lifts the shield for that app for 5 minutes |
| Activity monitor | `DeepFocusExtensions/ActivityMonitor` | Applies the shields when the session's schedule starts, lifts them when it ends, so a killed app can't leave them on |

The extensions can't reach the network (Apple says so for the shield configuration extension;
the other two are undocumented, so the design assumes the same, docs/decisions/0008): they write
slips to the App Group and the app sends them with `POST /sessions/:id/slip` when it next runs.

Sessions with under 15 minutes left get a backdated DeviceActivity interval that still ends with
the session; "Open anyway" lifts the shield for that app for 5 minutes (decision 0008).

## Switching it on (after Apple approves)

Apple's Family Controls (Distribution) approval comes per App ID: the app and each extension
(docs/apple-setup.md step 7). Until then the extensions build in CI but aren't in the app, and the
app has no Family Controls entitlement, so TestFlight uploads keep working. Once every App ID is
approved:

1. In `project.yml`, add `com.apple.developer.family-controls: true` to the Lockin target's
   entitlements and add the three extension targets to its `dependencies` (`- target:
   LockinShieldConfiguration` and so on), and change their scheme entries from `[test]` to `all`.
2. Push with `[testflight]`.

The app reads the entitlement at run time: without it, the Deep Focus row says it's waiting on
Apple instead of offering the picker.
