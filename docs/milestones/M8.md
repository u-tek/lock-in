# M8 — Deep Focus

Status: **built; switched off until Apple approves Family Controls**

## Scope

SPEC.md §5.8, Plus only: pick distracting apps with the Family Controls picker; during a session
they're shielded; "Open anyway" on a shield records a **slip**, which the squad sees (member state
5) and which dents the pet's mood.

## How it fits together

| Piece | Where | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `DeepFocus` module | `Packages/LKKit/Sources/DeepFocus` | Authorisation, the saved app selection, starting and stopping shields for a session, the slip inbox in the App Group |
| Shield configuration | `DeepFocusExtensions/ShieldConfiguration` | Draws the shield: the user's Lockling, "You're locked in", "Back to focus", "Open anyway" |
| Shield action | `DeepFocusExtensions/ShieldAction` | "Back to focus" closes the app; "Open anyway" records a slip and lifts the shield for that app for 5 minutes |
| Activity monitor | `DeepFocusExtensions/ActivityMonitor` | Applies the shields when the session's schedule starts, lifts them when it ends, so a killed app can't leave them on |

The extensions can't reach the network (Apple says so for the shield configuration extension;
the other two are undocumented, so the design assumes the same, docs/decisions/0008): they write
slips to the App Group and the app sends them with `POST /sessions/:id/slip` when it next runs.

Sessions with under 15 minutes left get a backdated DeviceActivity interval that still ends with
the session; "Open anyway" lifts the shield for that app for 5 minutes (decision 0008).

## Switching it on (after Apple approves)

Apple's Family Controls (Distribution) approval comes per App ID: the app and each extension
(docs/apple-setup.md step 7). Until then the extensions build in CI but aren't in the app, and the
app has no Family Controls entitlement, so TestFlight uploads keep working. Once every App ID is
approved:

1. In `project.yml`, add `com.apple.developer.family-controls: true` to the Lockin target's
   entitlements and add the three extension targets to its `dependencies` (`- target:
   LockinShieldConfiguration` and so on), and change their scheme entries from `[test]` to `all`.
2. Push with `[testflight]`.

The app reads the entitlement at run time: without it, the Deep Focus row says it's waiting on
Apple instead of offering the picker.

## As built (app)

- **Settings › Deep Focus** (`App/Sources/Settings/DeepFocusScreen.swift`), a row beside
  Notifications with a Plus crown for people without Plus. Without Plus: one line and "Get Lockin
  Plus" (paywall moment 3). With Plus, by Screen Time status: not entitled, "Coming soon";
  not asked, "Turn on Deep Focus" (Screen Time's own prompt, then the switch turns on); denied,
  "Allow Lockin in Settings › Screen Time" and Open Settings; approved, "Block apps during
  sessions" and "Choose apps" ("3 apps, 1 category", "None chosen") opening the Family Controls
  picker. Snapshot routes: `settings.deepfocus`, `settings.deepfocus.plus`,
  `settings.deepfocus.soon`.
- **`DeepFocusModels`** (`App/Sources/DeepFocus`) holds the authorisation, the controller, the
  selection, the switch (App Group defaults `LKDeepFocusOn`, off by default) and the selection's
  counts. Saving a choice tells the controller, so a running session shields it at once.
- **`DeepFocusCoordinator`**, owned by `LiveServices`, follows the session engine: with Plus,
  the switch on and Screen Time approved, a running session starts the shields (a squad's under
  the room's id, a solo one as `solo-<start>`), a pause lifts them, a resume (or a squad's moved
  end) puts them back until the new end, and the finish, or any of the three conditions going
  false, stops them. It stores the Lockling for the shield whenever it changes.
- **Slips**: at launch and on every return to the foreground the coordinator reads Screen Time's
  status again, reconciles the shields and drains the slip inbox. If any slip belongs to the squad
  session running now it calls `SessionEngine.slip()` once for the batch; slips from other
  sessions, or solo ones, are dropped. Each batch counts `deepFocus.slip` (`slips`, `squad`).
- **A slip is gentle** (docs/decisions/0009): slipped is an active state, so the timer keeps
  running, the Live Activity keeps the countdown ticking in pink, and the squad sees it for 5
  minutes. Solo, a slip changes nothing on the session.
- Not yet: a slip that can't reach the server is not put back in the inbox (the engine's
  `slip()` doesn't report back); the picker and shields are untested on a phone until Apple's
  approval arrives.
