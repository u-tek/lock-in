# M9 — Launch

Status: **Eng side built**; the rest is the owner's accounts, a TestFlight beta and the submission.
The working list is `docs/launch/checklist.md`; this page says what Eng built for it.

## Scope

From SPEC.md §6 and §7: privacy policy, support URL, App Review notes, demo accounts, TestFlight
with 20+ real students across several squads, analytics review, launch content plan.

## Built

- **Launch drafts** (`docs/launch/`): privacy policy, terms, support and FAQ, App Review notes,
  App Store listing, and the checklist. `[OWNER: …]` marks what only the owner can fill in.
- **Web pages**: the Worker serves `/`, `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/support` from those drafts,
  with placeholders highlighted and kept out of search engines until they're filled.
- **Links in the app**: the paywall's Terms and Privacy and Settings › About's Support, Privacy and
  Terms open those pages (`LK_WEB_HOST`); without a host they're hidden, never `example.com`.
- **Demo Buddy** (SPEC.md §7): the review code makes a private Review Squad with a server bot that
  joins the reviewer's sessions, cheers, pauses and finishes, so the squad Live Activity can be
  seen with one phone. `app-review-notes.md` §3.
- **App Store screenshots**: `store.1`…`store.5`, rendered on the iPhone 16 Pro Max by
  `[snapshots: store]` into `design/appstore/`.
- **The island on film**: `[island]` runs a scripted squad session in the simulator and records the
  real Dynamic Island and Lock Screen into `design/island/` (video and stills), for review and for
  the launch content plan's island clips.
- **Analytics**: decision 0010 sets out the options (proposed: no third-party provider, integer
  daily counts to our own `POST /metrics`). The app still logs counts locally until the owner
  decides.

## Not built (owner, or needs real phones)

Everything under "Owner" in the checklist: the Apple Developer setup, App Store Connect products
and agreements, RevenueCat, the domain and email, the Worker deploy and its secrets, the beta with
students, the age rating, and the submission. The beta's measurements (Live Activities started
against failed, join-to-island time, pushes per room, retention) come from TestFlight and the
server once real people use it.
