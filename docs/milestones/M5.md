# M5 — Pet, streaks, leaderboard, cosmetics

Status: **in progress**

## Scope

From SPEC.md §1.2, §1.5, §2 and §6: the squad pet hatches, grows and has moods; squad streaks;
a weekly squad leaderboard; coins become server-side once you're signed in; a coin shop for
Lockling cosmetics. Plus items stay Plus (paywall moment 3); Squad Boost and streak freezes are M7.

Done when: a squad's egg hatches after its first group session, the pet grows with focused minutes
and shows its mood in the Live Activity and the app, the streak counts and breaks by the rules
below, the leaderboard resets on Monday, and coins bought items survive a reinstall when signed in.

## Rules (shared: `fixtures/pet/rules.json`, tested by the server and by `Model`)

```json
{
  "species": ["puff", "sprout", "ember", "pebble"],
  "stageXp": [0, 0, 600, 3000, 10000, 25000],
  "streakMinMinutes": 20,
  "streakShare": 0.5,
  "sadMinutes": 30,
  "coinsPerMinute": 1,
  "completionBonus": 5
}
```

- **XP** is focused minutes: each member's whole focused minutes in a squad room are added to
  the squad's `pet_xp` when they finish or leave (early leavers' minutes still count).
- **Stages**: 0 egg, 1 baby, 2 teen, 3 adult, 4+ prestige. The egg **hatches** (stage 1) when a
  squad room first has two or more members who finished (`done`); before that XP still adds up.
  After hatching, the stage is the highest `n` with `xp >= stageXp[n]` (never below 1); stages
  past the table's end don't exist yet. Progress `x` = (xp − stageXp[stage]) / (stageXp[stage+1] −
  stageXp[stage]), clamped to 0–1, and 1 at the last stage; an egg's `x` is 0.
- **Species** is picked at random when the squad is made (so the egg can show its colour) and
  revealed at hatching. Existing squads get one on first read.
- **Mood** (0–3), for "today" in the squad's time zone:
  - 0 sad: someone slipped or left early in this squad within the last 30 minutes;
  - 1 hungry: nobody in the squad has finished a session today;
  - 2 content: someone has, but today doesn't count for the streak yet;
  - 3 happy: today counts.
- **Streak day**: today counts when at least half the squad (rounded up, minimum 1) finished at
  least one session of 20+ focused minutes today, solo or squad. Each counted day after
  yesterday's counted day adds 1; a gap resets it to 1. A streak whose last counted day is before
  yesterday shows as 0 (it's broken; M7's freezes will fill gaps).
- **Squad time zone**: the creator's device time zone when the squad is made, updated when the
  owner registers a device in a new one. Days and the weekly reset use it, so the whole squad
  shares one Monday.
- **Coins**: 1 per whole focused minute, plus 5 for finishing (as in M1), solo or squad,
  credited by the server when a member finishes or leaves (signed in). Signed out, the app counts them as it does now.

`ShopItem = { id: "hat.1", part: "hat", option: 1, price: 80, plus: false }`, from
`fixtures/shop/catalogue.json` (shared with the app): every non-default option of each part
(body, colour, eyes, hat), the Plus ones marked `plus` with price 0. Defaults (option 0) are
always owned and aren't listed.

- `POST /me/coins/import`: once per account, so coins and items earned signed out aren't lost;
  coins capped at 5,000, items must be free ones that exist.
- The content-state's `p` is present in every squad room from M5: the egg is `"<species>.0"`.
  It's refreshed when XP, stage or mood change, at priority 5 (a hatch is 10).
- `PATCH /me` with an `avatarCode` naming a part you don't own: 403 `item_not_owned`
  (Plus parts need Plus). Old codes already stored are left alone.

## App

- `Creature`: `PetView(code:mood:size:)` draws the egg and four original species at baby, teen and
  adult (prestige = adult with a glow), with mood in the eyes and mouth. No images; SwiftUI shapes
  only, so it renders in the Live Activity.
- `LiveActivity`: `PetRow` and StandBy draw the pet instead of the paw.
- Squad detail: the pet card (name, stage, progress, mood in words), the streak, and this week's
  leaderboard.
- Home with a squad picked: the pet and the streak above Lock in.
- Finished: XP added and the hatch moment ("Your squad's egg hatched!").
- Shop: from the coin count on Home. Lockling items by part, price in coins, Plus items open the
  paywall. The Lockling editor offers what you own.
- Coins: the server's balance when signed in; on first sign-in the local balance and items are
  imported once.

Snapshot routes: `squads.detail` (with pet, streak and leaderboard), `home.squad` (with pet),
`finished.hatch`, `shop`, `shop.bought`, `lab.pets` (every species × stage × mood).
