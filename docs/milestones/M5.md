# M5 — Pet, streaks, leaderboard, cosmetics

Status: **in progress**

## Scope

From SPEC.md §1.2, §1.5, §2 and §6: the squad pet hatches, grows and has moods; squad streaks;
a weekly squad leaderboard; coins become server-side once you're signed in; a coin shop for
Lockling cosmetics. Plus items stay Plus (paywall moment 3); Squad Boost and streak freezes are M7.

Done when: a squad's egg hatches after its first group session, the pet grows with focused minutes
and shows its mood in the Live Activity and the app, the streak counts and breaks by the rules
below, the leaderboard resets on Monday, and coins bought items survive a reinstall when signed in.

## Rules (shared: `fixtures/pet/rules.json`, tested by the server and by `Model`)

```json
{
  "species": ["puff", "sprout", "ember", "pebble"],
  "stageXp": [0, 0, 600, 3000, 10000, 25000],
  "streakMinMinutes": 20,
  "streakShare": 0.5,
  "sadMinutes": 30,
  "coinsPerMinute": 1,
  "completionBonus": 5
}
```

- **XP** is focused minutes: each member's whole focused minutes in a squad room are added to
  the squad's `pet_xp` when they finish or leave (early leavers' minutes still count).
- **Stages**: 0 egg, 1 baby, 2 teen, 3 adult, 4+ prestige. The egg **hatches** (stage 1) when a
  squad room first has two or more members who finished (`done`); before that XP still adds up.
  After hatching, the stage is the highest `n` with `xp >= stageXp[n]` (never below 1); stages
  past the table's end don't exist yet. Progress `x` = (xp − stageXp[stage]) / (stageXp[stage+1] −
  stageXp[stage]), clamped to 0–1, and 1 at the last stage; an egg's `x` is 0.
- **Species** is picked at random when the squad is made, so the egg shows its colour (a hint of
  what's inside); the app names it only once it hatches. Existing squads get one on first read.
- **Mood** (0–3), for "today" in the squad's time zone:
  - 0 sad: someone slipped or left early in this squad within the last 30 minutes;
  - 1 hungry: nobody in the squad has finished a session today;
  - 2 content: someone has, but today doesn't count for the streak yet;
  - 3 happy: today counts.
- **Streak day**: today counts when at least half the squad (rounded up, minimum 1) finished at
  least one session of 20+ focused minutes today, solo or squad. Each counted day after
  yesterday's counted day adds 1; a gap resets it to 1. A streak whose last counted day is before
  yesterday shows as 0 (it's broken; M7's freezes will fill gaps).
- **Squad time zone**: the creator's device time zone when the squad is made, updated when the
  owner registers a device in a new one. Days and the weekly reset use it, so the whole squad
  shares one Monday.
- **Coins**: 1 per whole focused minute, plus 5 for finishing (as in M1), solo or squad,
  credited by the server when a member finishes or leaves (signed in). Signed out, the app counts them as it does now.

## As built (server)

Details the app relies on, settled while building the Worker (449 server tests). No shape in the
contract above changed; everything here fills in what it left open.

**Coins, XP and the pet in the rooms**

- Credited by the room when each timer stops: finished (`done`), left early (`end` before time),
  slipped, or the 8-hour cap. Coins = whole focused minutes × 1, + 5 when finished; paused time
  doesn't count. Solo sessions earn coins too; only squad rooms add XP (the same whole minutes).
  Each timer is credited once: joining a room again after finishing is a new timer that earns
  again. Nothing tells the app the amount: it can work it out with the same rule (SessionRecord)
  for the finished screen, and `GET /me` has the balance.
- The egg hatches when two different members have finished a timer in the same squad room (one
  member finishing twice doesn't). XP before then still adds up. `hatched_at` is the second finish.
- Sad (mood 0) for 30 minutes after an early leave or a slip **in one of the squad's rooms**; a solo
  early leave, or the 8-hour cap, isn't sad.
- "Finished a session today" (hungry vs content) is any finished timer, solo or squad, of any
  length, by a current member; the streak needs 20 focused minutes or more.
- `p` is in every squad room's content-state from the start: an egg is `"<species>.0"` with
  `x: 0`, hungry until someone finishes. `x` is rounded to 2 decimals. M3.md's "`p` is absent until
  the pet hatches" no longer holds.
- `p` is refreshed after every finish, leave or slip in the room, when a sad spell ends, at the
  squad's midnight, and when a member finishes somewhere else (solo, or another squad's room): that
  room tells this one. Those are priority 5 (10 when it's the hatch), coalesced like any change;
  a stop in the room is priority 10 anyway. The final `end` push carries the pet after the last
  finish, hatch included.

**Streaks and days**

- Days are calendar days in the squad's `tz`. A finished timer counts on the day it stopped. A day
  counts when `doneToday >= needed`; `needed` = half the current members rounded up, at least 1.
  `count` includes today once it counts; it's 0 once the last counted day is before yesterday.
- The squad's `tz`: `POST /squads` takes an optional `tz` (IANA name; 422 `tz_invalid` otherwise),
  else the creator's most recently registered phone's zone, else `"UTC"`. The app should send it:
  a user who refused notifications has no phone registered. `POST /devices` moves every squad the
  caller owns to the phone's zone. Existing squads took their owner's latest phone's zone.
- A zone the runtime doesn't know counts as UTC.

**`GET /squads`**

- `pet.species` is null until hatched (as the contract says), even though the room's `p` names the
  egg's species. `pet.progress` is unrounded (`(xp − stageXp[stage]) / …`, so Model's
  `PetRules.progress` gives the same number); `pet.stage` and `pet.mood` match what `p` shows.
- A squad from before M5 gets its species the first time it's read.

**Leaderboard**

- `minutes` is the sum of each timer's whole focused minutes; `sessions` counts finished timers.
  Every timer of the member counts, solo and in any squad's room, in the week it stopped.
- `weekStart` is the Monday of `week` (any date in the week); `startsAt`/`endsAt` are Unix seconds,
  Monday 00:00 to the next Monday 00:00 in the squad's zone (`endsAt` exclusive). Without `week`,
  this week in the squad's zone. Every current member is listed, 0s included. Ties by display
  name (else handle, else "Lockling"), ignoring case, then by id.
- 422 `week_invalid` for anything but a real `YYYY-MM-DD` date; 403 `not_member` is checked first.

**Shop and cosmetics**

- `fixtures/shop/catalogue.json`: 20 items, ids `<part>.<option>` (`body.1`, `colour.6`, `eyes.4`,
  `hat.5`). Prices: colours 40–60, eyes 60–80, hats 80–120, bodies 120–180. Plus items: `body.4`,
  `colour.6`, `colour.7`, `eyes.3`, `hat.2`, `hat.5`.
- `GET /shop` needs no sign-in (the app can show the shop signed out).
- `owned` (in `GET /me/cosmetics`, `POST /shop/buy`, `POST /me/coins/import`) lists, in catalogue
  order, what you bought or imported **plus every Plus item while Plus is active**: exactly the
  non-default parts you may wear.
- `POST /shop/buy`: a non-string `itemId` is 400; checks in order 404 `item_not_found`, a Plus item
  (402 `plus_required` without Plus, 409 `already_owned` with it), 409 `already_owned`, 402
  `not_enough_coins`. Two purchases racing can't overspend or double-buy.
- `POST /me/coins/import`: `coins` must be a whole number ≥ 0 and `owned` an array of at most 100
  strings, else 422 `import_invalid`. Coins over 5,000 are cut to 5,000 and added to the balance
  the server already has. Unknown and Plus ids are skipped silently, duplicates and ones already
  owned too. An empty import (`{ coins: 0, owned: [] }`) still uses up the one import. The second
  call is 409 `already_imported`, whatever it carries.
- `PATCH /me` `avatarCode`: only the parts that **change** are checked, so an old code is left
  alone and changing one part keeps the others. Defaults (0) are always fine; an option the
  catalogue doesn't have is not owned. 403 `item_not_owned` carries `itemId`, the first part
  that isn't yours; nothing is saved.

**Storage**

- `migrations/0005_pet.sql` also adds `focus_log`: one row per timer that stopped, with what it
  earned. It's what makes crediting exactly-once and what streaks and the leaderboard read
  (participants keeps only someone's latest timer in a room). SPEC.md §4.6 should list it.
- `users.coins_imported` isn't in `User`; the app learns it from 409 `already_imported`.

## App

- `Creature`: `PetView(code:mood:size:)` draws the egg and four original species at baby, teen and
  adult (prestige = adult with a glow), with mood in the eyes and mouth. No images; SwiftUI shapes
  only, so it renders in the Live Activity.
- `LiveActivity`: `PetRow` and StandBy draw the pet instead of the paw.
- Squad detail: the pet card (name, stage, progress, mood in words), the streak, and this week's
  leaderboard.
- Home with a squad picked: the pet and the streak above Lock in.
- Finished: XP added and the hatch moment ("Your squad's egg hatched!").
- Shop: from the coin count on Home. Lockling items by part, price in coins, Plus items open the
  paywall. The Lockling editor offers what you own.
- Coins: the server's balance when signed in; on first sign-in the local balance and items are
  imported once.

Snapshot routes: `squads.detail` (with pet, streak and leaderboard), `home.squad` (with pet),
`finished.hatch`, `shop`, `shop.bought`, `lab.pets` (every species × stage × mood).
